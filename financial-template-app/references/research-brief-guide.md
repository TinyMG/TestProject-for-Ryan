# Research Brief Guide

How to fill `assets/research-brief-template.md` for different kinds of sample
URLs, and how to judge adopt vs. adapt.

## Contents

- Judging adopt vs. adapt
- By template type: what to extract
- Proposing default numbers
- Source quality

## Judging adopt vs. adapt

For every element the sample contains, make one explicit call:

- **Adopt as-is**: the element maps directly to a music school (e.g., a
  monthly revenue table, a fixed-cost list).
- **Adapt**: the element is right in spirit but needs translation (e.g., a
  SaaS "MRR per plan" table becomes "tuition per tier"; "churn" becomes
  "student cancellation rate").
- **Drop**: the element has no music-school meaning (e.g., CAC payback for
  ad-based SaaS, burn rate with venture funding). Say why in one line so the
  owner sees it was considered, not missed.

Rule of thumb: the sample decides *structure and rigor*; the school decides
*content and numbers*.

## By template type: what to extract

- **Business-plan / financial-projection page**: section list, projection
  horizon, which statements it includes (revenue, P&L, cash), granularity
  (monthly vs. annual), assumption style.
- **Spreadsheet-style budget**: column structure (line items x periods),
  formula patterns (which cells are computed vs. entered), subtotal style.
- **Pricing page**: tier structure, per-period pricing, fees, discount
  mechanics - these often map onto tuition tiers and registration fees.
- **Dashboard / one-pager**: which 4-8 numbers it leads with, how it groups
  KPIs, what levers it exposes.

## Proposing default numbers

1. Anchor on [LIVE] numbers re-verified from mirallesmusic.com this run:
   tuition tiers, registration fee, billing discounts.
2. Use profile facts next: 90 students/week, 6 teachers incl. owner, CAD,
   GST 5%.
3. Cross-check with benchmarks in `references/benchmarks.md`; if a benchmark
   conflicts with an assumption, note the tension in the brief.
4. Everything without a source is [ASSUMPTION] and must be editable in the
   app. Never present an assumption as fact.

## Source quality

Prefer, in order: the school's own live pages; other music schools' published
pricing; music-teacher associations and industry surveys; generic small-business
articles last. Record the URL and date for every benchmark used.
